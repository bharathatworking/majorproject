## Winner (pre-registered rule)

1. Eligible: mean FKGL drop ≥ 2 grades and near copies ≤ 10% of outputs.
2. Among eligible models, the highest meaning kept wins.
3. If the top two are within 3 percentage points, the faster CPU-only model (median s/100 words) wins.
4. The winner must be fine-tunable with Unsloth on a Colab T4; otherwise it is dropped and the rule is applied again.

- qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M has the highest meaning kept (85.0%), 5.5 percentage points ahead of gemma4:e2b-it-qat.
- qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M is fine-tunable with Unsloth on a Colab T4: it is the winner.

**Winner: qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M.**
